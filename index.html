How to Build This into a Live Production App

To turn this working prototype into a permanent live cloud app accessible on your team's smartphones and computers, choose one of these two recommended paths:

Option A: Microsoft Power Apps + SharePoint (Enterprise/Warehouse)

Upload this roster list into a SharePoint list or Excel file. Open Power Apps studio and connect it as a data source. It generates an enterprise-ready mobile & desktop app instantly.

Option B: Google Sheets + AppSheet (Fastest No-Code)

Put your team members into a Google Sheet with columns for Name. Go to AppSheet.com, connect the sheet, and it automatically builds a native app for iOS and Android.
